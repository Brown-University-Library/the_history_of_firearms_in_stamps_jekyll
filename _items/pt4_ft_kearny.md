Stephen Watts Kearny (sometimes spelled Kearney) spent his long and distinguished military career on the frontier. In the early 1840s, his forces provided military escort to settlers heading west on the Oregon Trail. In 1848, a fort was built in south-central Nebraska, and named in his honor. When the Colorado Gold Rush began in 1858, Ft. Kearny was the last stop before migrants would head southwest to Colorado, following the South Platte River. The stamp was issued on the centennial of the fort’s construction.
