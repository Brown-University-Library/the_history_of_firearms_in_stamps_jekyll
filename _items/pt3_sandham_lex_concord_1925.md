The American Revolution began at Lexington & Concord on April 19, 1775, when British soldiers marched out from Boston to conduct house-to-house searches for gunpowder and firearms. The British won a quick victory at Lexington, were repulsed at the Concord Bridge, and nearly wiped out on their march back to Boston, as armed Americans swarmed in from the countryside.

This 1925 stamp is based on the mural "The Birth of Liberty," by Canadian painter Henry Sandham. Issued in April 1925, it was part of the first group of stamps to honor the American Revolution, along with the Minuteman stamp and the Washington at Cambridge stamp.
