The stamp was issued on the bicentennial of the great expedition.

![A close-up of the lock of the Lewis & Clark Girardoni.](../img/itemimgs/girardoni2.webp)

Here is another view of the famous [Girardoni](https://www.nramuseum.org/guns/the-galleries/a-prospering-new-republic-1780-to-1860/case-8-romance-of-the-long-rifle/girardoni-air-rifle-as-used-by-lewis-and-clark.aspx).

The Girardoni was expensive, and worth it. Traveling through a country where the Corps of Discovery was far outnumbered, the expedition showed off the rifle’s repeating capabilities to astonished Indians. The friendly demonstrations made the implicit point that the smaller group could defend itself against the larger group.
